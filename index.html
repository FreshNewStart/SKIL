As we continue to progress with the ongoing works and prepare for the upcoming development phases, it is important to formalize the working arrangement through the execution of the required documentation. This ensures clarity of roles, protects both parties, and allows for smooth coordination with third-party service providers. To facilitate this, the following documents are provided for your review and execution:

Letter of Appointment & Authority

Purpose:

This document serves as a practical confirmation of our appointment and allows us to engage and coordinate with contractors, suppliers, and consultants on your behalf.

Why it’s important:

  • Enables efficient communication and coordination with third parties
  • Provides clear proof of authority when managing project activities
  • Supports smooth day-to-day execution without delays

Purpose:

This agreement formally appoints us as the exclusive developer for the estate-related works, specifically covering the 2 x 10-unit development, land tagging, and associated sales and development activities.

Why it’s important:
